# Iran tax office salary-list files (WH / WP / WK) - findings

Two OFFICIAL tax-office guides ("راهنمای تولید فایل الکترونیکی مالیات بر درآمد حقوق", portal salary.tax.gov.ir) were found, mirrored on accounting blogs (PDFs, fetched and read):

- NEW guide v1.7.0.0 dated 1403/02/27 (IT dept, سازمان امور مالیاتی). Mirror: https://www.hesabdary.com/@HesabdaryCom/23770/ (PDF in wp-content/uploads/2024/05/...1403@HesabdaryCom.pdf). OFFICIAL document, SECONDARY hosting.
- OLD guide (undated in extractable text; file title says "[W|D]HYYYYMM", has WK; example file WP138701; secondary source calls it version 10-13950215, i.e. ~1395). Mirror: https://hesabdari.co/Media/Uploads/tax/tax-salary-traning.pdf (page: https://hesabdari.co/article/2512/). OFFICIAL document, SECONDARY hosting. NOTE: digits in this PDF use a broken font map, so lookup-table codes are partly unreliable; column order/titles below are reliable.

KEY RESULT: the user's sample (WH = 34 columns, col 19 = employee insurance share, WP ~22 cols, WK exists) matches the OLD guide, not v1.7.0.0. v1.7.0.0 has WH = 35 columns (rial) and WP = 23 columns, and has NO insurance-share column and NO WK.

## Common rules (both guides)
- File names: WH + YYYYMM + .TXT (payroll detail), WP + YYYYMM + .TXT in new guide (WP personnel); old guide also WK + YYYYMM + .TXT (list summary). YYYY = 4-digit Jalali pay year, MM = 2-digit Jalali month (01 Farvardin ... 12 Esfand). Examples: WH140301.txt, WP140301.txt; old: WP138701/WK138701/WH138701. [OFFICIAL]
- Separator: comma (ASCII 44), no header, one record per line, line terminator {CR}{LF}. Empty optional field = nothing between two commas. [OFFICIAL]
- Encoding: UTF-8 (Windows). Old guide: DOS variant used "Iran System" encoding (file prefix D instead of W). Guide says nothing about Arabic vs Persian yeh/kaf. [OFFICIAL]
- Numbers: no thousands separators; new guide: negative numbers NOT accepted; old guide: negative values allowed for all payment items. Dates: YYYYMMDD, digits only, no separators (Gregorian vs Jalali not stated explicitly in text read; examples are Jalali-like e.g. 1387..). Amounts in rials; all payments of a month (rial/foreign/both) must go in ONE file; WK amounts always rial. [OFFICIAL]
- WP only needs sending once / for new or changed employees. [OFFICIAL]
- Three WH layouts exist depending on payment type (table "نوع پرداختی": 1 rial, 2 foreign, 3 rial+foreign): rial-only, foreign-only (same columns as rial, amounts in currency), and rial+foreign (every money column duplicated).

(Digit codes inferred from a garbled-font PDF; verify against a real accepted file. User's 22-col WP does not match exactly: it is probably a variant of this layout, one column fewer.)

## OLD guide - WH rial layout, 34 columns [OFFICIAL, hesabdari.co PDF]
1 national code, 2 payment type (1 rial/2 foreign/3 both), 3 actual months worked since start of year (1-12), 4 is this last month of employment (1 yes / 0 no), 5 currency code (rial = 85 per table; text says use the rial code), 6 FX rate (1 for rial), 7 start date, 8 end date (opt), 9 employee status (exemption code table), 10 workplace status (table), 11 gross recurring cash pay, 12 overdue recurring pay with no tax computed, 13 housing (1 none, 2 furnished, 3 unfurnished), 14 amount deducted from salary for housing, 15 vehicle (1 none, 2 with driver, 3 without), 16 amount deducted for vehicle, 17 recurring non-cash benefits, 18 medical expenses (art.137 DTA), **19 insurance premium paid (art.137 DTA) = employee insurance share**, 20 bank housing-loan facility exemption (art.139 / 3rd development plan), 21 other exemptions, 22 gross overtime, 23 other non-recurring cash pay, 24 one-off bonuses, 25 overdue non-recurring cash pay, 26 deduction: non-recurring cash exemptions (art.91 cl.6 travel), 27 non-recurring non-cash benefits, 28 eidi and year-end benefits, 29 leave and severance buyout, 30 deduction: art.91 cl.5 exemption, 31 free-trade-zone exemption (0 if none), 32 double-taxation exemption (0 unless status 7), 33 tax due on salary/non-cash/non-recurring/eidi/buyout for the month, 34 total net tax due for the month.
Foreign-only layout: same 34 columns, amounts in currency. Rial+foreign layout: rial and foreign columns interleaved (longer). Col 1 = national code, col 19 = insurance premium: matches the user's sample facts.
Sample WH line from the PDF has empty optional fields as consecutive commas.

## OLD guide - WK (list summary) 14 columns [OFFICIAL, hesabdari.co PDF]
1 year (4 digits), 2 month (2 digits), 3 current-month tax liability, 4 prior-months tax liability, 5 journal-entry date (allocation/payment) YYYYMMDD [required], 6 payment method (1 personal cheque, 2 POS/card, 3 bank transfer, 4 promissory note, 5 certified cheque, 6 cash deposit, 7 treasury deposit, 8 list without tax payment), 7 cheque serial (6 digits), 8 cheque date, 9 bank code (table), 10 branch name, 11 account number (max ~20-29 digits), 12 amount paid/cheque amount, 13 treasury payment date, 14 treasury paid amount. Cols 9-12 required for non-cash payment. Sample: WK138701.TXT.

## NEW guide v1.7.0.0 (1403) [OFFICIAL, hesabdari.co mirror]
- Files: WHYYYYMM.TXT, WPYYYYMM.TXT only (no WK; summary is now entered online / dropped). UTF-8, comma, CRLF, no header, no negatives.
- WP = 23 columns: 1 nationality (1 Iranian, 2 foreign), 2 national code, 3 first name, 4 last name, 5 father name, 6 birth date YYYYMMDD, 7 birth-certificate no., 8 birthplace, 9 education (1-7), 10 insurance type (1 medical services, 2 social security, 3 health insurance, 4 other, 5 none), 11 insurance number (10 digits), 12 insurance name, 13 exemption type (1-10), 14 country of nationality, 15 country of residence, 16 postal code, 17 address, 18 category/رسته (1-19), 19 job title (سمت), 20 employment type (1-12), 21 start date, 22 end date, 23 retirement date.
- WH rial = 35 columns: 1 national code, 2 payment type, 3 workplace status (1-4), 4 budget-law exceptions (1 normal,2 faculty,3 judges), 5 currency, 6 FX rate, 7 gross recurring cash, 8 overdue recurring cash, 9 housing, 10 housing deduction, 11 vehicle, 12 vehicle deduction, 13 recurring non-cash, 14 overdue recurring non-cash, 15 fees/consulting/attendance/teaching, 16 research contracts, 17 overtime, 18 travel cost, 19 mission allowance, 20 karaneh, 21 bonus (excl. year-end/end-of-service), 22 year-end bonus, 23 annual eidi, 24 end-of-service bonus, 25 dismissal compensation, 26 service buyout, 27 seniority pay, 28 unused-leave pay, 29 other non-recurring cash, 30 overdue non-recurring cash, 31 non-recurring non-cash, 32 overdue non-recurring non-cash, 33 health insurance premium art.137, 34 life insurance premium art.137, 35 net paid to employee. Foreign-only = same 35; rial+foreign = 59 columns.
- Insurance share is NOT in this layout (it was in col 19 of the old one).
