## 1. Eidi and annual bonus

- Basis: "قانون تعیین عیدی و پاداش سالانه کارگران شاغل در کارگاه‌های مشمول قانون کار", Majlis 1370/12/06, confirmed by Guardian Council. SECONDARY (search summary of https://www.ekhtebar.ir/قانون-تعیین-عیدی-و-پاداش-سالانه-کارگرا/ ; page itself did not load). It is a stand-alone Majlis law, not Labour Law art. 33 and not a cabinet decree. Government staff: separate 1374 law.
- Amount: 60 days of latest wage per year of work; capped at 90 days of the legal minimum daily wage. SECONDARY (same + https://sarayehesabdar.ir/1405-new-years-bonus-for-workerss/).
- Wage base (sarayehesabdar): daily wage + seniority base + job benefits; if none, daily wage. SECONDARY.
- Proration under 1 year: fixed daily wage x 60 x (days worked / 365). SECONDARY. Part-time: proportional to hours; seasonal/piece-rate: 60 days prorated by days worked. Tabsare 2: more generous workplace custom prevails.
- 1405 figures (SECONDARY, sarayehesabdar): min daily wage 5,541,850 rial; min eidi 332,511,000 rial (60 d); max 498,766,500 rial (90 d). Arithmetic checks. Verify the min wage against the 1405 Supreme Labour Council decree.
- Social insurance: NOT subject. Cited as Tamin circular 4195 section 3 clause (د). SECONDARY (https://bidbarg.net/blog/tax-sso-deductible/ , sarayehesabdar). Art. 34 of Social Security Law not examined.
- Income tax: Direct Taxes Law art. 91 clause 10: annual eidi/year-end bonus exempt in total up to 1/12 of the art. 84 annual exemption. With 480M toman annual exemption (1405) = 40,000,000 toman exempt; excess taxable. SECONDARY (https://nabzgheymat.ir/ article on 1405 salary tax; sarayehesabdar). Described as separate from the monthly 40M band. 1404 value NOT found.
- Payment date: no statutory date found; practice is before Nowruz (Esfand).

## 2. Hours, overtime, night, Friday

Source: law-text mirror https://edalatsara.com/rules/قانون-کار/مبحث-دوم-–-مدت-کار , .../ماده-62 , .../ماده-63 (SECONDARY).

- Art. 51: 8 h/day; 44 h/week.
- Art. 52: hard/harmful/underground work max 6 h/day, 36 h/week.
- Art. 55-56: shift work; shift allowances (10% morning+afternoon; 15% with a night shift; 22.5% some combos per summary - verify).
- Art. 58: night work (not art. 60): non-shift workers get +35% of normal hourly wage per night hour.
- Art. 59: overtime needs worker consent, +40% over hourly wage (factor 1.4); overtime assigned max 4 h/day except exceptional cases agreed by both parties.
- Art. 60: emergency overtime at employer's discretion (accident prevention, repair, restart after flood/earthquake), max 8 h/day, notify labour office within 48 h.
- Art. 61: no overtime for night workers or hazardous/hard/harmful work (mirror summary; verify).
- Art. 62: Friday is the paid weekly rest day. Tabsare 1: where another fixed rest day applies (public services, nature of work, agreement) that day is the rest; working the rest day pays +40%. Tabsare 2: week under 6 days -> rest pay = 1/6 of that week's wages. Tabsare 3: 5-day/44 h week gives two paid rest days at daily wage.
- Art. 63: 11 Ordibehesht (Workers' Day) is an official holiday for workers; no premium stated.
- Holiday (non-Friday) work at 40%: practice, statute not directly confirmed.
- Overtime is subject to both tax and insurance (SECONDARY, bidbarg).

## 4. Employee loans

- Deduction cap: it is Labour Law art. 44 (not 42): for a worker's debt to the employer only the part above minimum wage, by court order, never above 1/4 of total wage. SECONDARY (https://edalatsara.com/rules/قانون-کار/ماده-44).
- Art. 45: employer may deduct only in listed cases, including instalments of employer loans (clause ج), advances, calculation overpayments. SECONDARY (wikihoghoogh via search summary).
- Loan write-off: NO explicit rule found. Not listed in the circulars seen. Whether it is salary under art. 83 or insurable wage: UNVERIFIED (likely taxable benefit is my inference only).

## 5. Sanavat

- Art. 24: one month of last wage per year of service (30 x daily wage). SECONDARY.
- Fractions: daily wage x 30 / 365 x days worked is the common formula; 360 not found in any source. SECONDARY (https://sarayehesabdar.ir/right-of-years/ and other search results).
- Base: a Administrative Justice Court ruling reportedly makes base the total wage incl. art. 34/35 components (ruling number not found). SECONDARY.

## 6. Two salary levels in one month

One row vs two rows for a mid-month change: NOT VERIFIED. Suggested engine approach (my inference): compute each segment by days and sum into one row.

## Other 1405 facts (SECONDARY)

- Salary tax monthly (toman, nabzgheymat.ir): to 40M exempt; 40-80M 10%; 80-100M 15%; 100-120M 20%; 120-140M 25%; over 140M 30%. Annual thresholds 480M/960M/1.2B/1.44B/1.68B. Circular 200/1405/59 dated 27 Mehr 1405 (allowances).
- Employee insurance 7% (exempt from salary tax per ruling 1415 of 2021-06-02); employer about 20% + 3% unemployment.
